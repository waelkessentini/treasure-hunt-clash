Treasure Hunt Clash

Up to four treasure hunters, one chest of gold. Each round everyone secretly plays one card, then all the cards flip at once. The first to 10 gold wins. Friends can join from their own phone or computer; empty seats are played by AI.

    How to play

    Everyone secretly picks one card and presses Lock In. When the whole table has locked in, all cards flip together. Digs and dice resolve first, then steals. Gold is your treasure score: first to 10 gold wins. If several players pass 10 in the same round, the highest total wins.

      Fine print

      • •Gold dug this round can be stolen this round. Each thief takes up to 2.
      • •Two thieves on the same target each take up to 2. If the target has less, they share it one coin at a time.
      • •A thief who hits a Guard pays the guard 1 gold, if they have any left after the steals.
      • •Rolling 1–2 with no gold costs nothing. Gold never drops below 0.
      • •Roll your die before you lock in. One roll per Double Dig.
      • •Sudden death: if the highest total at 10+ is shared, nobody wins yet. Play continues until one player alone holds the highest total at 10 or more.
      • •Lantern: once per game, before the reveal, peek at one rival's face-down card. A friend's card can be peeked once they have locked in.
      • •Turn timer: if the host set one and time runs out, an unlocked player guards (or plays the Double Dig they already rolled).
      • •If a friend leaves, an AI takes their seat and gold. They can rejoin with the same code.
